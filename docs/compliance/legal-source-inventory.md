## 1. Overview

This document catalogs all legal, statutory, and regulatory reference documents governing the **Luxera Provenance** financial evidence and compliance intelligence platform.

As an enterprise financial compliance application serving financial institutions, wealth managers, and Islamic fintechs operating in Southeast Asia (specifically Malaysia/ASEAN) and international jurisdictions, Luxera Provenance is bound by strict statutory requirements regarding personal data protection, anti-money laundering, and automated financial decision-making transparency.

## 3. Statutory Data Processing Principles (Act 709 Mapping)

1. **General Principle (Section 6):** Personal data shall not be processed without data subject consent or statutory exemption (e.g. AML legal obligation).
2. **Notice & Choice Principle (Section 7):** Data subjects must be informed via a written Privacy Notice regarding purpose of SoW processing, classes of third parties (e.g., n8n engine, cloud database), and contact details.
3. **Disclosure Principle (Section 8):** Data shall not be disclosed for any purpose other than SoW verification without explicit consent.
4. **Security Principle (Section 9):** Practical, technical, and organizational measures must protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction.
5. **Retention Principle (Section 10):** Data shall not be kept longer than necessary (subject to AMLA 7-year statutory retention override).
6. **Data Integrity Principle (Section 11):** Data must be accurate, complete, and up-to-date.
7. **Access Principle (Section 12):** Data subjects retain the right to access and correct their personal data.
