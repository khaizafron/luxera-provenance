P.U. (B) 522 

# AKTA PERLINDUNGAN DATA PERIBADI (PINDAAN) 2024 

# PENETAPAN TARIKH PERMULAAN KUAT KUASA 

PADA menjalankan kuasa yang diberikan oleh subseksyen 1(2) Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi (Pindaan) 2024 [ _Akta A1727_ ], Menteri menetapkan— 

- _(a)_ 1 Januari 2025 sebagai tarikh seksyen 7, 11, 13 dan 14 Akta itu mula berkuat kuasa; 

- _(b)_ 1 April 2025 sebagai tarikh seksyen 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 10 dan 12 Akta itu mula berkuat kuasa; dan 

- _(c)_ 1 Jun 2025 sebagai tarikh seksyen 6 dan 9 Akta itu mula berkuat kuasa. 

Bertarikh 19 Disember 2024 [PDP.100-1/1/2(11); PN(U2)3315] 

GOBIND SINGH DEO _Menteri Digital_

P.U. (B) 522 

PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2024 

# APPOINTMENT OF DATE OF COMING INTO OPERATION 

IN exercise of the powers conferred by subsection 1(2) of the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 [ _Act A1727_ ], the Minister appoints— 

- _(a)_ 1 January 2025 as the date on which sections 7, 11, 13 and 14 of the Act come into operation; 

- _(b)_ 1 April 2025 as the date on which sections 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 10 and 12 of the Act come into operation; and 

- _(c)_ 1 June 2025 as the date on which sections 6 and 9 of the Act come into operation. 

Dated 19 December 2024 [PDP.100-1/1/2(11); PN(U2)3315] 

GOBIND SINGH DEO _Minister of Digital_
